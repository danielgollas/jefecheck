The Core Player more details...
 JefeCheck is an Image Sequence Player that allows you to play High Resolution (SD, HD, 2K+) on almost any workstation, includingsome pretty old Mac laptops (2K frames at 24fps on a 1GHz G4 Mac laptop anyone?)

Real Time Processing more details...
JefeCheck allows you to take the image sequences you are playing back and apply image processing filters on them (FXs we call them), at full resolution and in Real Time. No need to render the FX, JefeCheck makes full use of the enormous processing power inside your computer's video card to perform all these tasks on the fly.

Apply 1D and 3D LUTs, do quick and dirty bluescreen chroma keys, do primary color correction, extract difference mattes on the fly, mix stereo pairs into anaglyphs without thinking about it twice.

If you are slightly technologically inclined, you can write your own FXs with extreme ease (FXs are written in the OpenGL Shading Language).

Online colaboration more details...
So you are playing back your files and applying all kinds of neat filters, but playing alone is no fun... start a JefeCheck Remote Session Server and have your team participate from their own JefeCheck workstation, even if they are thousands of miles away, everything you do is also done on their station, anything they do is done on yours.

It's like being next to each other fighting over the mouse.
