JefeCheck License Store

Welcome to the JefeCheck store, here you will be able to buy JefeCheck licenses via PayPal using your PayPal account or a credit card.

 

  1. Use this page to buy one ore more licenses (you need one license per computer)

  2. After you purchase a license, you will receive an email with a link to download the JefeCheck installation package and a Purchase Confirmation Number.

  3. Go to the download page and enter your Purchase Confirmation Number to download and Install JefeCheck

  4. Start JefeCheck, you will be prompted to find a license or request a new one. Click on the request new license button and fill out the required information, including your Purchase Confirmation Number.
    You will need a working internet connection to do the request, but you will only need to do it once. (If you don't have an internet connection, you have the option to send the request by email and we will send you the license in no more than two workdays).

  5. You will receive your license by mail in a few minutes. Next time you start JefeCheck, when prompted for a license, point to the license file you received by mail. You're done!
