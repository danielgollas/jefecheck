SHARE *

  • Participate in JefeCheck Remote Sessions: playback and review sequences over a network or the Internet with as many participants as you want.
  • Control all aspects of the playback from any workstation in the session.
  • Apply any FX on any computer in the session, what you see is what everyone else sees.
  • Hold remote sessions between Windows, OS X and Linux workstations.
  • Secure: no image information travels over the network, frames must be synchronized on both computers beforehand.
  • Use remote pointers and text chat to communicate exactly what you want to all other participants.

*Remote Sessions over the Internet require at least one participant to have a public IP address and control over the Firewall if any.
