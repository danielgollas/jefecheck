FFmpeg
======

This distribution bundles a static build of FFmpeg (https://ffmpeg.org),
used by JefeCheck's video-export feature to encode rendered image
sequences into H.264 / H.265 / ProRes files (run as a separate process).

JefeCheck also LINKS the FFmpeg libraries libavformat, libavcodec,
libavutil, libswresample and libswscale to read .mov/.mp4 movies and their
sound. Packaged builds use a GPLv3 FFmpeg (--enable-version3), so
distributed JefeCheck binaries are effectively under GPLv3.

The bundled binary is a GPL build obtained from:
  https://github.com/eugeneware/ffmpeg-static  (release b6.1.1)

FFmpeg is free software licensed under the GNU General Public License
version 2 or later (GPL builds enable GPL-only components such as x264 /
x265). JefeCheck is distributed under the GNU GPL, version 2 or (at your
option) any later version, so this combination is license-compatible.

FFmpeg source and license texts are available at https://ffmpeg.org and
in the upstream ffmpeg-static release. FFmpeg is a trademark of Fabrice
Bellard, originator of the FFmpeg project.

You may replace the bundled binary with your own ffmpeg build, or point
JefeCheck at a different one via Preferences → Render → FFmpeg path (or the
JEFECHECK_FFMPEG environment variable).
